## Names that refer to the same feature

- FireArc, 小火龍, and 小火龙 name the same assistant.
- Topic Patrol, Topic Tracking, Coast Guard, 海巡, and 海巡大師 refer to the
  same topic-discovery and reply feature.
- Lead List, Lead CRM, 客戶名單, and 糊口名單 refer to customer lead lists.
- Traffic Stage, Watchtower, 流量搭台, and 流量塔台 refer to the post-performance
  area. Navigation and page headings use different variants of its name.

A Business Profile describes a business; a Creator Profile describes a creator.
Accounts are connected social identities; Connected apps are external services
that the user has authorized to access ViralArc.
